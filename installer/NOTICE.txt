OMNIX 4 — native Office rebuild preview

This installer adds a native OMNIX ribbon and docked workspace to supported Windows desktop editions of Excel, Word and PowerPoint.

Close Office before installing. Microsoft VSTO Runtime is included and may request administrator permission if it is missing. Microsoft may ask you to trust each OMNIX Office add-in. If your organization blocks development add-ins, installation stops; OMNIX does not change that policy or install a root certificate.

This is a development preview. Windows build and automated component checks do not prove operation in your Office installation. Production approval requires real Office, restart, provider and signing evidence.

API keys are encrypted for your Windows account. The initial privacy setting asks before every remote request. No documents are sent during installation.
